\section{Declarations}
\label{sec:declarations}

\subsection{Data availability}
The study is designed around publicly available benchmark datasets, including TartanAir, the Blackbird UAV Dataset, MARSIM, and AeroVerse, subject to their respective licenses and redistribution terms. Dataset versions, selected sequences, conversion parameters, and SHA-256 manifests used for the final experiments will be frozen before confirmatory evaluation. Derived benchmark summaries and machine-readable result tables used in the article will be released with the public repository when the study is finalized.

\subsection{Code availability}
The reference implementation, benchmark adapters, experiment configuration, metric computation, and scripts used to generate the reported tables and figures are maintained in the public S4D-TAM repository. The publication release will be tagged and archived with a persistent identifier before submission of the final manuscript.

\subsection{Conflict of interest}
The author declares no conflict of interest.

\subsection{Funding}
Funding information will be stated here before submission. If the work was not externally funded, this subsection shall be replaced with an explicit statement that no specific funding was received for this research.

\subsection{Ethics approval}
This work concerns autonomous robotic systems and public or appropriately licensed benchmark data and does not involve human participants or animals. No human-subject or animal ethics approval is therefore required for the benchmark study as currently designed.
